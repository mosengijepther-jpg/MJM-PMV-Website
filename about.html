About MJM Enterprise Limited

MJM Enterprise Limited is a Papua New Guinea-based business providing transportation services to passengers and communities.

The company operates a PMV transportation service connecting passengers travelling along the Lae-Gabensis route.

Our Transportation Service

Our PMV service is designed to support passengers travelling between communities by providing accessible transportation.

The Nembah Sewek PMV is part of the company's transportation operation and serves passengers travelling along the designated route.
